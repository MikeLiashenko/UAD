# Google Play — store listing texts

Paste into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing (default language
Ukrainian; add Russian and English under "Manage translations" → "Add your own translations").
Limits: name 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000.
Check the lengths with `node store/check_lengths.js`.

## en-US

### Name
Ukraine Air Defense (UAD)

### Short description
Command an air-defense battery and guard the skies of Kyiv, Kharkiv and Odesa

### Full description
You command an air-defense battery. By day you hunt recon drones; by night you hold the sky over the city against Shahed swarms, cruise missiles and ballistic missiles. Every decision counts: where the battery stands, what you fire at each target and where the debris comes down.

★ FOUR REAL CITIES
Kyiv, Kharkiv, Dnipro and Odesa on their real coordinates: districts, rivers, bridges, ports, power plants and landmarks stand where they really are. The map is 30 × 30 km, and one unit is one metre.

★ FIFTEEN AIR-DEFENSE SYSTEMS
From a DShK machine gun on a pickup and the ZU-23-2 to the Gepard, Osa, IRIS-T, NASAMS, Buk, S-300 and Patriot. Every system has its own range, ceiling and radio horizon. A Shahed is brought down with a gun, not a million-dollar missile.

★ SIX WAYS TO FIGHT
Command from the map — or take the weapon yourself: the gunner's sight, an FPV interceptor drone, an F-16 sortie, a mobile fire group on a pickup.

★ A LIVING CITY
Debris physics, fire crews who put out fires and rebuild, blackouts when a power plant is hit, the morning report, rewards, fines and the budget.

★ STRIKING BACK
The radar traces launches back to their sites — buy a strike, and less will come from there the next night.

★ MAXREALISM MODE
The defense through the eyes of one person on the position: no map, no markers. Walk between the vehicles, take a seat at a sight or an operator's console and listen to the radio.

★ CO-OP
A friend code, a join request — and one city you defend together over the internet. An account is optional.

★ MADE FOR TABLETS AND PHONES
Full touch control: the stick, the fire and alarm buttons each follow their own finger. Graphics from old phones up to powerful tablets.

• Free, with no ads and no purchases
• Single player works offline
• Ukrainian, Russian and English
• Every model, sound and texture is generated by the game's code

The game is in Alpha: updates come out often.
